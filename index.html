About Me

A final-year student who thinks in tables and charts.

I'm a final-year BS Computer Science student with a growing specialization in data analytics, business intelligence, and data-driven decision-making. What draws me to this field is simple: data holds answers, and I like being the person who finds them.

Over the past two years I've built a strong foundation in Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI, statistics, and data visualization — applying them to real datasets covering fraud detection, crime trends, streaming content, and retail sales. I care about getting the analysis right, not just making it look polished.

A big part of that work happens before any chart gets built: cleaning and validating messy, real-world data so it can actually be trusted. From there I've extended into machine learning, training and evaluating models like Logistic Regression and Random Forest to turn cleaned data into predictions, not just summaries.

I'm currently seeking a Data Analyst internship or entry-level opportunity where I can keep sharpening these skills on real business problems.
